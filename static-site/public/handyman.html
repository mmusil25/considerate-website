Questions

How much does a handyman cost in Columbus?

I charge $59/hr with a $125 minimum, which covers up to 2 hours. Most jobs get a fixed price instead, quoted from photos. The 3-hour Honey-Do block is $165. For comparison, solo handymen in Columbus typically charge $55–$95/hr and franchise companies $75–$125/hr.

When are you available?

Weeknights 5:30–9 pm · Sat & Sun 12–6 pm. This is my evening and weekend business, so you can book work without taking time off.

What size jobs do you take?

Small interior jobs, $125 to about $600: a few patches, a room of paint, a regrout, a list of small fixes. Larger remodels are better served by a full-time contractor, and I'm happy to refer you.

Are you insured?

Not yet. I'm a new, one-person business and don't carry general liability insurance yet. To keep risk low, I only take small interior jobs (patching, painting, caulk and grout, trim and minor repairs) and no electrical, plumbing, roofing, structural or exterior work. All workmanship comes with a 1-year guarantee: if a repair fails because of my work, I come back and fix it free.

Do I need to be home?

For the first visit, yes. For multi-visit jobs like a second drywall coat or grout, many clients leave a lockbox or door code.

What areas do you serve?

Columbus within about 20 minutes of North Linden (43211): Clintonville, the University District, Short North, Westerville, Worthington, Gahanna, Bexley and nearby.

Serving Clintonville, Linden & North Columbus, University District, Short North & Italian Village, Westerville, Worthington, Gahanna, Bexley, and the rest of Columbus within about 20 minutes.
