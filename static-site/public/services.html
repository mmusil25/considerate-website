How engagements work

Hourly. Starting at $120/hr. Best for advisory work, debugging, and small, well-scoped tasks.

Retainer. A reserved block of hours each month. Best for ongoing maintenance, fractional-engineer support, and being on call when hardware misbehaves.

Fixed price. A quoted price against an agreed specification. Best for well-defined deliverables — a prototype, a website, a test fixture, a model.
