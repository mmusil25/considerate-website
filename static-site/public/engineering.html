Capabilities

Embedded systems & firmware

Embedded Linux (OpenWRT, PetaLinux, Debian), bare-metal and RTOS firmware in C/C++, Xilinx Zynq SoC/FPGA in VHDL and Verilog, sensor buses (SPI, I2C, Modbus, RS-485), and IoT fleets over MQTT.

Electrical, PCB & RF engineering

Schematic capture and PCB layout in Fusion 360, antenna design and characterization, WiFi/LTE/Bluetooth radio design and test, and lab verification with VNAs, spectrum analyzers and oscilloscopes.

Systems engineering (MBSE)

Requirements, SysML and UML architecture in Cameo/MagicDraw and Papyrus, and reverse engineering of existing C++ codebases into maintainable models. Experience at the system-of-systems level on radar and counter-drone systems.

Web, cloud & DevOps

Websites and web apps (React, Next.js, Payload CMS, WordPress), deployed on infrastructure you own: Terraform, Docker, AWS, or self-hosted servers, with CI/CD and ongoing maintenance.

Machine learning & signal processing

Computer-vision detection models (custom YOLO), speech-to-text pipelines, time-series anomaly detection for predictive maintenance, and classical DSP for small-signal and radar work.
