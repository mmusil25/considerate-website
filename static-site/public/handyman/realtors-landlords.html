Pre-listing & inspection repairs

Work from the inspection report or your walk-through notes. Each item is quoted separately so it can go into negotiations.

Rental turnovers

Patch and paint, caulk, replace hardware, hang blinds, swap smoke detectors. Done between leases on evenings and weekends, so units turn faster.

Photo documentation

Before and after photos of every item, sent as a single summary. Useful for security deposit records and for clients who can't be there.

How it works

  1. Send photosUse the quote form or text me photos and a short description. No need to schedule a site visit.
  2. Get a fixed priceMost small jobs get a firm price from the photos. Bigger ones get a range and a quick look in person.
  3. Pick an eveningWeeknights after 5:30 or weekend afternoons. You get a reminder the day before.
  4. Pay when it's doneCard, Apple Pay, Venmo, Zelle, check or cash. Jobs over $300 with special-order materials need a materials deposit.

Questions

Can you work from an inspection report?

Yes. Send the report and mark the items you want done. I'll quote each item separately so you can decide what gets fixed and present it to buyers and sellers.

Do I need to be there?

No. Lockbox or code access is fine. You get before and after photos of every item, plus a short summary you can forward to your client.

Do you offer a rate for repeat work?

Property managers and agents who send regular work get priority scheduling and a single monthly invoice. Ask about it.

Have a listing or a turnover coming up?

Send the punch list or inspection report and get an itemized quote.
