Interior finishing is my day job. I'm an apprentice with a professional interior finishing company, and I take the small finish jobs they don't want, on evenings and weekends: trim that's been missing since the flooring went in, a door that sticks, gaps that need caulking.

Trim & Interior Finishing: common questions

How much does baseboard installation cost?

Usually $5–$7 per linear foot of labor, depending on profile and corners, with a $125 minimum. Most single rooms come to $210–$380.

Can you fix a door that won't latch or sticks?

Yes. Most sticking or unlatched doors are fixed within the $125 minimum by adjusting the hinges, the strike plate, or planing the door.
