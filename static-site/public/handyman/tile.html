Tile, Grout & Caulk Repair: common questions

How much does it cost to regrout a shower in Columbus?

Regrouting and re-caulking a standard tub surround starts at $250. Replacing up to five cracked tiles starts at $190 if matching tile is available.

Do you fix leaking showers?

I can regrout and re-caulk, which fixes most surface leaks. If the waterproofing behind the tile has failed, that's a full rebuild. I'll tell you honestly and point you to the right contractor.
