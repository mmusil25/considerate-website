Pricing questions

Why are these 'starting at' prices?

Every house is different. These prices cover the typical case. Send photos and you'll get a firm price before any work starts, and the price won't change unless you add to the job.

How are materials billed?

At cost + 15% to cover pickup and handling, with receipts available. You're welcome to supply your own materials.

Is there a trip charge?

No trip charge within my service area. The $125 minimum covers travel and up to 2 hours of work.

How do I pay?

By card or Apple Pay through a secure payment link, or by Venmo, Zelle, check or cash. Jobs over $300 with special-order materials need a materials deposit upfront.

Have a repair in mind?

Send a few photos and I'll text or email back a price, usually within 24 hours.
