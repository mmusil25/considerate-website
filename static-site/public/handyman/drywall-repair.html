How I work

Joint compound needs time to dry between coats, so most patches take two short visits: patch and first coat on one evening, then sand, finish and prime the next. That fits my evening schedule well, and you aren't paying for me to stand around waiting for mud to dry.

Drywall & Plaster Repair: common questions

How much does drywall repair cost in Columbus?

Small patches (up to about 6 inches) start at $145, including texture match and primer. More patches in the same room are $34 each. Larger repairs up to about 2×2 ft start at $250. Large water-damage or ceiling replacements are bigger than I take on right now, and I'll gladly refer you.

Do you repair plaster walls?

Yes. Many homes in Clintonville, Bexley, Worthington and the University District have original plaster. I repair cracks and small holes so they don't come back: loose plaster is secured, cracks are taped, and the area is skimmed to blend with the wall around it.

Will I be able to see the patch?

The goal is no. I feather the compound wide and match the existing texture. On smooth walls under strong side light, a fresh coat of paint on the whole wall gives the cleanest result, and I can quote that too.

Do you paint after the patch?

Every patch is primed. If you have the leftover paint, touching up the patch is included. Otherwise I can paint the whole wall corner to corner so the sheen matches. See painting.
