Interior painting in Columbus

I take on the painting jobs that are too small for a big crew: one bedroom, a hallway, an accent wall, a set of doors, a stained ceiling. Prep is most of the work. I fill and sand, caulk the gaps, cut clean lines, and leave the room the way I found it.

Interior Painting: common questions

How much does it cost to paint a room in Columbus?

A standard bedroom (up to about 12×12 ft, walls only, two coats) starts at $380 in labor. Ceilings add about $125 and doors are $38 each. Paint is billed at cost, or you can supply your own.

Do you paint exteriors or whole houses?

No. I focus on interior rooms and small projects, which are a good fit for evening and weekend work. For whole-house or exterior painting, I'm happy to refer you to someone.

How long does one room take?

Usually two evenings or one weekend afternoon plus an evening, depending on prep and the number of coats.
